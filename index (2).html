About Me

Every image tells a story.

I am a photographer who believes a great photograph is made of light, composition and feeling. From the sparkle of a handcrafted product to the quiet drama of a portrait or the calm of a misty landscape, I create images that feel elegant and authentic.

My work is built on careful planning, attention to detail and clear communication, so clients receive polished, ready-to-use visuals that elevate their brand.

FAQ

Common Questions

How long until I get my photos?

Most shoots are edited and delivered within a few days of the session. Larger projects are agreed with you beforehand.

How many revisions are included?

Each package includes free revisions. Extra changes beyond that can be done for a small additional charge.

What file formats will I receive?

You receive high-resolution JPG files, plus web-sized versions for social media on request.

Do you shoot for small businesses?

Yes! I love helping small brands and makers look beautiful and professional online.

How do I book and pay?

Send me a message with your idea. I will reply with a quote and payment details.
